Beyond the numbers

Fisher and Buffett cared about things filings can’t show: the moat, the management, the customers. Claude can give a short qualitative read. This sends one small request (just the summary numbers, not the filing), so it uses only a sliver of your Claude usage.

Check or fix the numbers

Pulled automatically from the SEC file. Dollar amounts are in millions; per-share amounts in dollars. If a value is blank or looks wrong (it happens with banks and unusual companies), type the number from the 10-K and every score updates.

Verdicts are rule-based readings of each investor’s published criteria, applied to annual 10-K figures. They are not those investors’ actual opinions and not financial advice. Data comes from SEC XBRL filings; companies that report under international rules (most 20-F filers) aren’t supported.

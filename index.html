Meet the Panel

Who are these eight investors, and what do their tests actually measure? Pick an investor to learn his story and every number on his checklist, explained step by step with examples.

Every example uses PepsiCo’s real numbers from its 2024 annual report, rounded so the math is easy to follow. Where a stock price is needed, the examples pretend PepsiCo’s shares cost $150; see Score a Stock for today’s real price and numbers.

These explanations describe each investor’s published rules as this site applies them. They are for learning, not financial advice.

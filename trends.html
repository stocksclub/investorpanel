Trends

Pick a company and an investor to see how the numbers he cares about have changed over the years, next to the company’s sector.

Share of his tests passed, year by year

Each point scores the company as if that year’s report were the latest. Early years have less history behind them, so some long-term tests can’t be scored yet.

Charts use each company’s annual 10-K reports. Price-based numbers (like P/E) use the share price at the end of each fiscal year and are available for S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and Dow companies. Earlier years are adjusted for stock splits so per-share numbers line up. Sectors are built from the S&P 500 companies in them, weighted by market value, the way sector funds like XLK are built.
